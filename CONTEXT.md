# PrayerPal

PrayerPal helps friends and family support one another in completing the five daily prayers. This glossary defines the product's domain language; it is intentionally separate from implementation details.

## People and groups

**Prayer Circle**:
A group of friends and family who agree to share completed-prayer activity with one another for encouragement. A circle can be found by name and member count, joined through an invite link, and has equal permissions for all members; its prayer activity is visible only to members.
_Avoid_: Community, network

**Circle Member**:
A person who belongs to a Prayer Circle and can see that circle's shared prayer activity. Members have the same permissions; the person who created the circle has no special authority.
_Avoid_: Follower, participant

## Prayer activity

**Obligatory Prayer**:
One of the daily obligatory prayers represented by PrayerPal: Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, or Isha. On Friday, Jumu'ah replaces Dhuhr in the tracked daily set.
_Avoid_: Task, activity

**Prayer Window**:
The period associated with one Obligatory Prayer, beginning at its configured local start time and evaluated using the user's device timezone.
_Avoid_: Deadline

**Timing Configuration**:
The named set of location, calculation method, juristic school, and adjustments used to determine a user's Prayer Windows.
_Avoid_: Prayer schedule, fixed times

**Prayer Outcome**:
The user's selected result for an Obligatory Prayer: completed, not completed, or Qada. A Prayer Outcome is initially finalized as not completed at local midnight, but can be changed for any date at any time.
_Avoid_: Status, post

**Prayer Completion**:
A user's declaration that they completed an Obligatory Prayer during its Prayer Window.
_Avoid_: Proof, verification

**Qada Prayer**:
A user's declaration that they completed an Obligatory Prayer later to make up for missing its Prayer Window. A Qada Prayer contributes to consistency-based rewards but not to perfect on-time rewards.
_Avoid_: Late prayer, retry

**Jumu'ah Prayer**:
The Friday congregational prayer tracked in place of Dhuhr for that day's Obligatory Prayer set.
_Avoid_: Friday prayer, Jummah

**Prayer Completion Notification**:
A configurable notification sent when a Circle Member records a Prayer Completion.
_Avoid_: Starting notification, alert

**Encouragement**:
A supportive reaction or message a Circle Member can send after seeing another member's shared Prayer Completion.
_Avoid_: Nudge, judgment

**Circle Message**:
A message visible to all current members of a Prayer Circle.
_Avoid_: Group chat, announcement

**Circle Invite Link**:
A single-use shareable link generated by a Circle Member that expires after two days, lets an existing user join the circle, or prompts a new user to sign up before joining. Any member can revoke an unused link.
_Avoid_: Join request, public link

**Circle Message**:
A message visible to all current members of a Prayer Circle.
_Avoid_: Group chat, announcement

**Direct Message**:
A private message between two people that remains available independently of their shared Prayer Circle membership.
_Avoid_: Private prayer activity

**Immutable Message**:
A Circle Message or Direct Message that cannot be edited or deleted by its author.
_Avoid_: Temporary message, editable post

**Circle Activity History**:
The completed-prayer activity that Circle Members can review for today, the current week, and the current month. Qada details and not-completed outcomes are not shown as circle activity.
_Avoid_: Public history, attendance record

**Circle Archive**:
An empty Prayer Circle that is retained but hidden from search after its final member leaves.
_Avoid_: Deleted circle, inactive group

**Account Deactivation**:
The user's account is removed from circles while their prayer history and rewards are archived for possible restoration if they return. Their messages remain in place with their display name.
_Avoid_: Permanent deletion, account deletion

**Active Prayer Location**:
The one location currently used to calculate a user's Prayer Windows. Users may change it manually.
_Avoid_: Automatic location, travel location

**Free Product**:
The complete PrayerPal experience is available without subscriptions, memberships, or advertising.
_Avoid_: Freemium, premium plan

## Progress and encouragement

**Prayer Ring**:
A visual representation of completion progress across the day's tracked Obligatory Prayers for a user or Prayer Circle.
_Avoid_: Score, level

**Consistency Streak**:
A consecutive measure of days on which the user completes every tracked Obligatory Prayer, including when a Prayer is recorded as Qada.
_Avoid_: Perfect streak

**Perfect Prayer Streak**:
A consecutive measure of days on which every tracked Obligatory Prayer was completed during its Prayer Window, with no Qada Prayer.
_Avoid_: Streak, flawless score

**Circle Celebration**:
A group-level celebration triggered when every Circle Member has completed all of that day's tracked Obligatory Prayers, including Qada completions.
_Avoid_: Leaderboard, competition

**Daily Prayer Badge**:
A daily reward for each tracked Obligatory Prayer: gold when completed during its Prayer Window and silver when completed as Qada.
_Avoid_: Score, grade

**Weekly Prayer Streak**:
The number of consecutive days in a week-based period on which the user completes every tracked Obligatory Prayer.
_Avoid_: Weekly score

**Monthly Prayer Streak**:
The number of consecutive days in a month-based period on which the user completes every tracked Obligatory Prayer.
_Avoid_: Monthly score

**Annual Perfect Prayer Reward**:
A special in-app reward for completing every tracked Obligatory Prayer during its Prayer Window on 365 consecutive days.
_Avoid_: Trophy, leaderboard prize

**Hanafi Timing Configuration**:
Prayer-time settings that initially use the Hanafi jurisprudential convention and one named calculation method supported by PrayerPal's timing provider. The complete calculation configuration, including the rules used for dawn and nightfall, must be named explicitly rather than inferred from the word Hanafi alone.
_Avoid_: Universal Islamic time, fixed time
