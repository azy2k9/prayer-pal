# Give circle members equal permissions

PrayerPal will not assign special permissions to the member who created a Prayer Circle. Every member may approve name-based join requests, generate or revoke invite links, remove another member, change the circle's name, photo, description, and discoverability, and leave freely. Circle creation establishes the group but does not establish ownership.

This matches the intended friends-and-family model, where the circle is a shared space rather than an organisation with a leader.
