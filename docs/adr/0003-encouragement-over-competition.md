# Reward encouragement without competition

PrayerPal will reward individual completion, consistency, and long-term practice through daily badges, weekly and monthly streaks, circle celebrations, and a special annual reward. It will not provide rankings or leaderboards: circle members can encourage one another, but the product will not compare their worship publicly.

This preserves the social motivation that differentiates PrayerPal while avoiding a competitive framing of worship. Gold and silver badges distinguish on-time and Qada completion without treating Qada as a failure.
