# Treat Qada differently from perfect prayer completion

PrayerPal will allow users to record Qada Prayers. A Qada Prayer preserves the user's consistency streak, but it does not preserve the Perfect Prayer Streak, which requires all tracked daily Obligatory Prayers to be completed without a missed or Qada Prayer.

This rule also applies to reward tiers: Qada earns the silver Daily Prayer Badge, while an on-time completion earns the gold badge. This separates ongoing effort from the stricter measure of an uninterrupted, on-time daily practice. The app records the user's chosen prayer outcome and does not issue religious rulings about when or how Qada should be performed.

Prayer outcomes roll over to not completed at midnight in the user's device timezone, but users may update any prayer on any date. Rewards and streaks are recalculated from the updated history rather than preserved as stale achievements.
