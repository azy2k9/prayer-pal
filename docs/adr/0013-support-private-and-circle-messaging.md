# Support private and circle messaging

PrayerPal will support both Direct Messages between two people and Circle Messages visible to all current members. Direct Messages remain open and retain their history independently of circle membership. A removed member loses access to the circle and its messages, while remaining members retain Circle Messages. Messages cannot be edited or deleted. Messaging notifications will follow the user's configurable notification settings; message visibility remains distinct from prayer activity visibility.

Private messaging enables personal encouragement, while Circle Messages support shared celebrations without requiring users to leave the prayer context.
