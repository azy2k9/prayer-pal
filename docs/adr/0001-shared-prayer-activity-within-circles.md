# Share prayer completions within private circles

PrayerPal will notify every member of the relevant Prayer Circle when a member records a Prayer Completion. The product is intended to create mutual encouragement among trusted friends and family, so membership—not public discoverability—is the boundary for prayer visibility. The initial version does not send “about to start” notifications, does not reveal whether a completion was recorded as Qada, and does not show not-completed outcomes as circle activity. Every notification type is configurable by the receiving user.

## Considered Options

- Share prayer activity publicly or through a discoverable network.
- Keep prayer activity completely private.
- Notify every member of an explicitly joined Prayer Circle when a member completes a prayer, while allowing each recipient to configure notifications and keeping Qada hidden.

The third option was chosen because it supports accountability without turning worship into public performance.
